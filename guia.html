A step-by-step guide to adding your country. No coding knowledge needed: you only edit text.

🧭 How it works

The website is already built. Every country page uses the same design. You only fill in one content file with your text and pictures, and the page builds itself (in your country's flag colours!).

You will create or touch only these things:

WhatWhere
Your card on the Home pagejs/paises.js (one small block)
All your page contentcontenido/yourcountry.js
Your picturesimages/yourcountry/
⛔ Don't edit any other file (pais.js, main.js, style.css, the .html files). If you want a design change, ask Shade.

1Get ready

  1. Install VS Code (free): code.visualstudio.com
  2. Get the MundoHispano folder from Shade (zip file) and unzip it.
  3. In VS Code: File → Open Folder… and choose the MundoHispano folder.
  4. In File Explorer, double-click index.html. The website opens in your browser.
💡 Keep the browser open. After every change: save in VS Code (Ctrl + S), then refresh the browser (F5) to see it.

2Add your card

  1. On the Home page, click the dashed ➕ Tu país aquí card.
  2. Choose your country, write your name, a short Spanish slogan, and your photo (for example images/mexico/portada.jpg).
  3. Click Crear tarjeta. Your card appears with a "Borrador" (draft) label.
  4. Click 📋 Copiar código.
  5. In VS Code open js/paises.js and paste it under the line // ↓↓↓ Paste your country here ↓↓↓. Save.
  6. Back on the Home page, delete the draft card with its ✕. Your permanent card stays.
📝 Remember the id in the code you pasted (e.g. id: "mexico"). You need it in the next step.

3Create your content file

  1. In VS Code, open the contenido folder on the left.
  2. Right-click espana.js → Copy, then right-click the contenido folder → Paste.
  3. Rename the copy to your id + .js, for example mexico.js.
  4. Click your card on the Home page. You now see a page with España's example text, in your colours.
⚠️ The file name must match your id exactly: lowercase, no accents, no spaces. "republicadominicana" → republicadominicana.js

4Write your content

Open your file. Change the text between the quotes. Here is what each part does:

PartWhat it is
datosQuick facts boxes (capital, language, currency…)
introduccionA short introduction under the facts
seccionesAll your sections. Add, remove or reorder them freely
reflexionYour "Lo que aprendí" paragraph

Section styles

Each section has a tipo that sets its look:

tipoLooks likeGood for
"tarjetas"Cards with picture + title + textPlaces, food, festivals
"texto"Paragraphs next to one pictureMusic, dance, history
"lista"Icon + title + textTransport, money, tips, fun facts
"frases"Flip cardsUseful phrases

Adding more

To add another card, copy one line inside items: [ ] and change it:

items: [
  { titulo: "Tacos", foto: "images/mexico/tacos.jpg", texto: "..." },
  { titulo: "Mole",  foto: "images/mexico/mole.jpg",  texto: "..." },   ← new
]

To add a whole new section, copy a full block from { to }, inside secciones.

5Your 25 Spanish words

Write Spanish words naturally inside your sentences using this pattern:

[[spanish word|english meaning]]

Example:

texto: "México tiene muchas [[playas|beaches]] y [[pirámides|pyramids]]."

The word is highlighted and shows its English meaning on hover. Use at least 25 different Spanish words across your page.

💡 Use them naturally in your sentences, not as a random list at the bottom.

6Add your pictures

  1. Create a folder images/yourcountry/ (e.g. images/mexico/).
  2. Save your photos there with simple names: tacos.jpg, playa.jpg (no spaces).
  3. Write the path in your file: foto: "images/mexico/tacos.jpg"

No picture yet? You'll see a 📷 Añade una foto box. That's fine, nothing is broken.

7Send your work to Shade

When you're done, send these 3 things (zip them together):

  1. Your content file: contenido/yourcountry.js
  2. Your pictures folder: images/yourcountry/
  3. Your card code from Step 2 (the block you pasted in paises.js)

Shade adds them to the main website. Because everyone has their own file, nobody's work gets overwritten.

✍️ Golden rules

  • Keep the quotes " " around every text. Need quotes inside a text? Use ' instead.
  • Keep the commas , between items and after every }.
  • Write it yourself. The assignment says: original work, no copy-paste. Research, then write in your own words.
  • Your reflection must be your own paragraph about what you learned and felt.
  • Replace all the "(Ejemplo)" text from España.

🔧 Something broke?

ProblemFix
All cards disappeared from the Home page A mistake in js/paises.js. Check quotes and commas in the block you pasted.
My page says "Página en construcción" Your file name doesn't match your id, or it's not in the contenido folder.
My page is blank or shows only the banner A missing quote or comma in your content file. VS Code shows a red squiggly line near the mistake.
My photo doesn't show Check the name exactly: .jpg vs .jpeg vs .png, and capital letters.
A word isn't highlighted Check the pattern: two square brackets, a | in the middle: [[sol|sun]]
🕵️ Still stuck? In the browser press F12 → Console. The red message shows the file name and line number of the mistake.

✅ Final checklist

  • ☐ My card shows on the Home page with my name
  • ☐ My page opens and uses my country's colours
  • ☐ No "(Ejemplo)" text left
  • ☐ I used at least 25 different Spanish words
  • ☐ My pictures show
  • ☐ I wrote my own reflection
  • ☐ I sent my 3 things to Shade
